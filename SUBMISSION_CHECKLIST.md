# Hackathon submission checklist

Complete these items before final submission and MASTER PASS. Do not mark an item verified without evidence.

## Repository and release
- [ ] Confirm the public repository URL and task-branch changes are ready for the owner to publish; do not include credentials or private data.
- [ ] Audit public-facing source, documentation, screenshots, and video for secrets and personal data. Keep real credentials out of Git and media.
- [ ] Run the production build in the project environment and record the result.
- [ ] Verify the public demo URL without secrets; record URL, date, and observed demo path. If unavailable, identify the owner-controlled blocker.
- [ ] Confirm the described behavior matches the shipped implementation. Do not claim banking/EMI status, PayPal replacement, internal settlement, or seller payout unless implemented and verified.

## Demo assets
- [ ] Record screenshots of the recommendation and PayPal Sandbox flow; label synthetic/demo content.
- [ ] Record and review the product video against [`DEMO_SCRIPT.md`](DEMO_SCRIPT.md); keep its final runtime between 2:00 and 2:30.
- [ ] Add final screenshot/video asset links and verify that they are accessible to judges.

## Hackathon submission fields
- [ ] Project title and concise description.
- [ ] Team/member names and contact details required by the submission form.
- [ ] Public repository URL.
- [ ] Live demo URL and access instructions, if applicable.
- [ ] Product video URL and screenshots/assets.
- [ ] Technology stack and accurate AI/PayPal integration description.
- [ ] Any required track, category, challenge, or sponsor fields.
- [ ] Confirm submission deadline and submitter-owned form requirements.
